## 2. What changed in the instrument

On perf/step-ledger, commit 7b1bee5c4 on origin. The ledger's own
contracts passed the throwaway smoke on the new semantics (interval
attribution, `forward_ms`, `cached_tokens`, the guard). The scheduler
path part of that smoke needs an environment with the pinned sglang
importable and did not run on the laptop, so the two forward marks in
`base.py` are reviewed, not executed, until the rerun's first window.

- interval fields (`cycle_ms`, `idle_sleeps_per_step`, `allocations_per_step`)
  belong to the step whose launch opened the interval, the last step of a
  run has none
- `forward_ms` from two more timing events around the model forward in
  `model_runner/base.py` `_prepare_and_forward`, hooks before and sampling
  after stay outside
- `cached_tokens` on extend rows from `batch.prefix_lens`
- the log line carries `forward` and `cached`

In doc 15: one profiled pass per concurrency with the benchmark's default
warmup instead of a warm pass plus a profiled pass, the Qwen3-TTS queue
knob for 64, and the MiniMax helper no longer needs `bc`.

## 5. Findings, ranked by the time they explain

### 5.1 Qwen3-Omni at concurrency is admission bound, not step bound

At c16 the talker holds each request 950 ms in its waiting queue before
prefill, 41 percent of the 2314 ms p50. At c32 it is 2107 ms of 3764. The
talker never runs above 9 rows although its row limit is 32
(`qwen3_omni/stages.py:1151`) and its pool held 21373 tokens at boot
(serve log, `KV Cache is allocated ... #tokens: 21373`), with requests
that need about 180 tokens each.

The path, file and line anchored:

```
thinker stream done
  -> talker request build ready          talker_scheduler.py:70 _is_request_build_ready
  -> waiting_queue                       event scheduler_queue_enter
  -> PrefillAdder.add_one_req            sglang schedule_policy.py:1201
       need  = extend_len + min(max_new_tokens, 4096) + page_size        :1218-1224
             = 128 + 4096 + 1 = 4225      talker max_new_tokens 4096, request_builders.py:1086
       have  = rem_total_tokens                                          :664
             = pool free - sum over running of
               min(max_new_tokens - output, 4096) * new_token_ratio      :655-661
       ratio = 0.7 * schedule_conservativeness at start                  environ.py:518, tracker :21-32
               minus (0.7 - 0.098) / 600 per step without a retract      scheduler.py:3554
               reset to 0.7 whenever the loop idles                      omni_scheduler.py:704
       admit iff need < have                                             :1236
  -> scheduler_prefill_start
```

With k running: have = 21373 minus k times (2867 reserved plus about 200
used). need 4225 < have holds for k at most 5, so the sixth request enters
and the seventh waits. As the ratio decays the cap drifts to 8 or 9, which
is the rows 6 to 9 spread in the c16 and c32 ledgers. The thinker gets
`max_new_tokens 256` from the benchmark (`benchmark_omni_seedtts.py:199`)
and a 5248 token pool, which allows about 16 rows, matching its rows 17
steps at c32 and its 110 ms queue wait there.

The decode rows say the device would take more rows cheaply: the talker at
8 rows spans 10.5 ms against 5.6 at one row. This is the observed KV
question of doc 14 section 4 with its number attached, and it has an
upstream control: `schedule_conservativeness` scales the initial ratio.
Task T15 runs the A/B.

### 5.2 Six of nine decode loops are device bound at c1 with about 1 ms of exposed host

Thinker, talker, Qwen3-TTS, Qwen3-ASR and MOSS-TD wait on the device for
most of the cycle. Their exposed host is 0.7 to 1.5 ms per step and the
device idle floor is 0.01 to 0.81 ms. The runtime seams of doc 14 section
3.4 are worth at most that much per step on these five. The other four
runners are unattributed until the rerun with `forward_ms`.

### 5.3 The per step cost is flat in rows on every TTS and music runner but the talker

Section 4. For Qwen3-TTS the device span is 6.7 ms at one row and 8.1 ms
at 16, for Higgs the host is 4.4 ms at one and 4.3 at 16. The cost is a
chain, not a batch: either a latency bound kernel sequence (Qwen3-TTS,
wait 5.6 ms says the device is the one taking the time) or host work per
step. `forward_ms` splits the graph replay from the rest on the rerun.

### 5.4 The talker's device span grows 1.3 ms per row

5.6 ms at one row, 13.2 ms at seven (c32), 14.3 ms at eight (c16), with
wait 7 to 10 ms, so it is device time. A batched decode of a small model
does not scale like that. Read task T16 on
`qwen3_omni/talker_model_runner.py`, the code predictor and the sampler.

### 5.5 Qwen3-ASR's coalesce hold is a fifth of latency at c8 and c32

Queue wait before prefill: 15.2 ms avg at c8 and 18.6 ms at c32, p95 at
41.9 and 41.7 ms, the 40 ms deadline (`qwen3_asr/stages.py:27`). Extends at
c32 are 16 to 18 rows and replay the prefill graph in 5 to 6.5 ms of host
with zero wait. The decode is device bound and 32 rows cost 1.4x one row.
This is the baseline for the derived gate A/B (T12): the hold buys prefill
batching on a stage whose prefill step is not the constraint.

### 5.6 MOSS-TD is clean and its request build costs more than its prefill

Device bound at 1.12 ms per step, 15 rows at 1.73 ms. The request build
runs 4.9 ms per request on the scheduler thread at c1 and 5.3 ms at c16,
against a 4.3 ms prefill step, and the coalesce gate produced 57 extends
for 100 requests at c16 (rows 1 to 3 mostly). Build placement is task T17.

### 5.7 MiniMax Music 3 is bound by the DiT stage, and its AR step is unattributed

dit_dav averages 15.0 s per song and 30.1 s at p95 in the five at once
pass, against 9.3 s of AR decode. The AR step is 12.2 ms at rows 2 and
13.2 at rows 10, flat, with one explicit stream synchronize and one token
list copy inside its post decode (`minimax_music3/model_runner.py:406`,
`:277`). `forward_ms` on the rerun says how much of the 12 ms is the
graph.

### 5.8 Smaller items

- Talker request build: 2.1 ms per request at c1, 13.5 ms at c16, 15.9 ms
  at c32, inline on the scheduler thread, 673 to 795 ms per window. Doc 14
  section 5.1.
- Talker to code2wav stream chunk hop: 1.2 ms avg at c1, 1.8 at c16, 11.3
  avg and 101 p95 at c32 over 2264 chunks. The relay backs up at c32. It
  does not move the offline benchmark, it moves streaming first audio.
- Higgs codec decode graphs are captured for frame counts 1 to 150
  (serve log 14:37:45), and 10 requests with more frames fell to eager
  decode during the runs, the vocoder p95 of 46 ms against 13 ms avg at
  c16.
- Allocations per decode step with a graph replaying: MOSS-TTS Delay 172
  to 615, MOSS-TTS-Local 94 to 102, MiniMax 49, thinker 34 to 90, talker
  23 to 92, Qwen3-TTS 34 to 77, Higgs 14 to 42, Qwen3-ASR 12 to 45, MOSS-TD
  10 to 25. The no churn assertion (T3) has its numbers.
- The thinker's steps at c16 cost 2.4x their c1 cost at the same rows.
  That is device sharing with the talker and code2wav on one H100, the
  price of the colocated topology, not a loop cost.
