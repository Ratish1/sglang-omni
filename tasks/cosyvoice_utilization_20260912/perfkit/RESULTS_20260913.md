# Perfkit slices of the 2026-09-13 H100 captures

Source: `artifacts/cosyvoice-results-20260913/cosyvoice/profile-*/trace.sqlite`, sliced with
`perfkit/slice_trace.py` on the analyzer's HTTP cohort windows. All times are exported Nsight
nanoseconds rounded to milliseconds. Profiler-on timings describe the traced cohorts; the
profiler-off full-corpus results are in `artifacts/cosyvoice-results-20260913/cosyvoice/full-matrix-report.md`.

Captures used: GPU 0 eager 16-request cohorts (`profile-{streaming,buffered}-en-c{1,16}`),
GPU 1 eager and compile cohorts (`profile-{eager,compile}-{streaming,buffered}-en-c{1,16}-gpu1`,
the 16-request `-qualified` streaming pair, the 32-request buffered pair, the 64-request compile
streaming run, the 32-request cold buffered pair). Warmup preceded every capture; the GPU 1
qualified pair used warmup 2 and is a cold path (first-use work is visible in it).

Facts:

- Decode GPU time is flat in batch: 1.02 ms at batch 1, 1.27 ms at batch 16. The decode graph
  replays 291 to 316 node kernels of 3 to 4 µs each; it is latency bound, not throughput bound.
- Sampling launches 31 to 35 eager kernels per step for 0.09 to 0.14 ms of GPU time and 0.6 to
  1.1 ms of host time.
- The `tolist` D2H range is 0.44 to 1.25 ms: it is the wait for the forward and sampling to finish.
- GPU busy fraction while the AR has admitted requests: 0.22 to 0.40 across captures.
- Custom eager prefill: 344 to 351 eager kernels, 1.5 ms of GPU time, 21 to 38 ms of host time
  (60 to 112 µs per launch). Prefill host cost is per launch, not per request: batch 8 costs the
  same host time as batch 1 except for the one-off sampling stalls.
- One-off sampling stalls of 20 to 109 ms appear on the first prefill of a new batch size in every
  capture, after warmup. Their owner is not identified by these traces.
- SM Active while only AR decode kernels are present: 12 to 17 percent.

## 4. Flow calls: launch bound below batch 4, GPU bound above batch 8

| path | batch | frames | calls | host ms | GPU union ms | GPU/host | host µs per kernel | kernels | mean kernel µs | syncs per call |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| native eager (streaming hop, final) | 1 | 200 to 600 | 19 + 18 | 273 to 350 | 64 to 89 | 0.20 to 0.31 | 15 to 19 | 18145 | 3.9 to 5.3 | 20 |
| native compile | 1 | 200 to 600 | 92 | 109 to 166 | 33 to 45 | 0.25 to 0.41 | 14 to 21 | 7780 | 5.2 to 6.7 | 20 |
| packed causal eager | 2 | 200 | 3 | 692 to 757 | 75 to 80 | 0.10 to 0.12 | 38 to 42 | 18209 | 4.4 to 4.7 | 23 |
| packed causal eager | 4 to 5 | 400 | 3 | 282 to 316 | 137 to 171 | 0.43 to 0.60 | 15 to 17 | 18290 | 7.9 to 9.8 | 31 |
| packed causal eager | 10 | 400 | 1 | 476 | 367 | 0.77 | 26 | 18433 | 21.0 | 55 |
| packed causal eager | 14 | 400 | 2 | 491 to 492 | 408 to 409 | 0.83 | 27 | 18533 | 23.0 | 71 |
| packed buffered eager | 1 | 200 to 800 | 3 | 283 to 846 | 68 to 100 | 0.08 to 0.24 | 16 to 47 | 18156 | 4.1 to 5.8 | 18 |
| packed buffered eager | 10 to 13 | 400 | 3 | 576 to 812 | 376 to 475 | 0.50 to 0.69 | 32 to 45 | 18168 | 21.7 to 27.2 | 54 to 71 |
| packed buffered compile | 1 to 2 | 200 to 400 | 7 | 174 to 266 | 35 to 52 | 0.13 to 0.30 | 22 to 34 | 7818 | 5.2 to 7.5 | not counted |
| packed buffered compile | 9 | 400 | 1 | 257 | 158 | 0.62 | 33 | 7826 | 22.5 | not counted |

Facts:

- One eager Flow solve is 18.1k to 18.5k kernel launches (10 Euler steps of the 22-block DiT plus
  setup). At batch 1 the mean kernel is 4 to 5 µs and the host needs 15 to 19 µs per launch, so the
  GPU is busy for a quarter of the call.
- torch.compile halves the launch count (7.8k) and leaves the call launch bound at batch 1.
- Packed calls at batch 10 to 14 are GPU bound (mean kernel 21 to 27 µs). SM Active while packed
  Flow kernels are present: 58 to 86 percent at batch 4 and above, 45 to 48 percent at batch 1 to 2.
  SM Active while native batch 1 Flow kernels are present: 15 to 25 percent.
- Every packed call issues `cudaStreamSynchronize` 4 times per batch row plus 15 per call
  (23, 31, 55, 71 for batch 2, 4, 10, 14). The per-row copies are the `.to(device)` of the CPU
  token, prompt token, prompt feature and embedding tensors in `_pack_flow_inputs`
  (`stages.py:165-185`); the per-call copies include the CPU noise prefix
  (`stages.py:403-408`) and the CPU length tensors of `token2wav_chunk` (`stages.py:1154-1163`).
  Sync host time inside a batch 14 call: 170 ms of 491 ms.
- The vocoder thread's host cost per launch doubles while the AR thread is active: the batch 2
  packed calls that ran during AR decode cost 38 to 42 µs per kernel against 15 to 17 µs for the
  same code after the AR finished (buffered GPU0: 47 µs for the batch 1 call at t=0.36 s).

## 5. HiFT calls: sync bound

| batch | frames | calls | host ms | GPU union ms | GPU/host | host µs per kernel | kernels | syncs per call | sync host ms |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|

| 1 | 200 to 400 | 11 | 72 | 6.6 | 0.09 | 61 | 1178 | 85 | 1.0 |
| 1 | 400 | 1 | 60 | 9.1 | 0.15 | 51 | 1182 | 85 | 1.1 |
| 1 (during AR) | 0 | 1 | 141 | 5.0 | 0.04 | 121 | 1168 | 86 | 28.2 |
| 5 | 400 | 1 | 123 | 33.9 | 0.28 | 105 | 1170 | 85 | 19.5 |
| 10 to 12 | 200 | 3 | 101 to 116 | 43 to 54 | 0.43 to 0.47 | 86 to 98 | 1172 to 1182 | 85 | 39 |
| 12 | 400 | 1 | 148 | 83 | 0.56 | 125 | 1182 | 85 | not shown |

Facts:

- A HiFT call is 1150 to 1190 kernel launches and about 85 `cudaStreamSynchronize` calls. The
  syncs come from host tensors moved to the device on every call: the Hann window in both STFT and
  ISTFT, the fixed sine and noise prefixes of the source module, and the float64 conversion of the
  f0 predictor (`generator.py:491-505,713-726`; `generator.py:208-226,338-375` per report 12).
  The exact sync count per source site needs the Python-sampled capture in the perfkit protocol.
- Streaming HiFT runs once per request per hop on the full accumulated mel; at batch 1 it is
  0.09 to 0.17 GPU busy and 13 to 15 percent SM Active. HiFT at batch 10 to 12 reaches 51 percent
  SM Active.

Facts:

  c16 the vocoder is behind the AR, so peers are already ready and the 30 ms windows rarely run.
