## The hop, for reference

```text
AR tokens:  [prompt, padded to a multiple of 25 | 25 hop tokens | 3 lookahead | ...]

hop k   flow.inference(tokens[: offset + hop + 3], streaming=True, finalize=False)  model.py:427-435
        mel[:, :, offset * 2:]        earlier frames recomputed, then dropped        model.py:436
        HiFT over all mel so far, crop speech[speech_offset:]                        model.py:447-449
        offset += hop; hop = min(100, hop * 2)                                       model.py:359-360
final   flow.inference(all tokens, streaming=False, finalize=True)                   model.py:366-373
```

Reference: FunAudioLLM CosyVoice `CosyVoice2Model.tts` and `CosyVoice3Model.token2wav` at
074ca6dc (`inputs/external_sources/CosyVoice/cosyvoice/cli/model.py`). Ported to Omni in #1656
(guozhihao-224, merged 2026-09-07); Omni's hop math is `models/fun_cosyvoice3/streaming.py`.

- Fixed by training: 50 frame chunk causal attention (`chunk_size: 25` tokens times
  `token_mel_ratio: 2`, cosyvoice3.yaml:14,17,74), a 3 token lookahead conv
  (upsample_encoder.py:82-105), the prompt padded so hops align to chunks.
- Not fixed by training: the prefix recompute, the 25, 50, 100 growth ("to avoid duplicate
  inference", model.py:411), and HiFT over the whole history.
- Why the prefix is expected to be stable (E5 decides): fixed noise indexed by frame
  (flow_matching.py:200,222), lookahead conv reads at most 3 tokens ahead, conv position embedding
  padded on the left only (DiT/modules.py:135), chunk causal attention (DiT/dit.py:163-164), every
  other module per frame.
- The final call stays a full bidirectional recompute: a causal final dropped the last 0.5 s cosine
  to about 0.29 (note at stages.py:1463-1465 on the stack).
